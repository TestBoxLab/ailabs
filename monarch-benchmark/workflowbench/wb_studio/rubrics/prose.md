# Prose — what the lab's writing must not do

From Wikipedia's *Signs of AI writing*, narrowed to what a benchmark report can actually get
wrong, plus the lab's own standing corrections. Cite a finding as `prose:<id>`.

## P1 Inflated significance
No "stands as a testament", "plays a vital role", "underscores its importance", "highlights the
significance". A result is interesting because of its number, not because a sentence says so.

## P2 Promotional language
No "rich heritage", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "robust", "powerful", "state-of-the-art",
"leverage", "unlock". We are measuring a product, not selling one.

## P3 Editorialising
No "it is important to note", "it is worth mentioning", "notably", "importantly". If it is
important, write it; if it is not, delete it.

## P4 Vague attribution
No "industry reports suggest", "experts say", "studies show", "observers note". Every claim names
its run, its task, its event, or its source.

## P5 The rule of three
No three-item lists used as decoration: "faster, cheaper and more reliable". A subtitle made of
three comma-separated nouns is the commonest form and is never acceptable.

## P6 Section summaries that repeat the section
No paragraph under a heading that restates the heading before saying anything. No closing
sentence that summarises what the reader just read.

## P7 Negative parallelisms
No "it is not merely X, but Y", "this is not just about X — it is about Y".

## P8 Em-dash and middot habits
No middots as prose separators. An em dash is fine once in a while; three on a page is a tell.

## P9 Hedging that hides a number
No "appears to perform somewhat better". Either the interval excludes zero or it does not; say
which.

## P10 Title case and bold scattering
Sentence case for headings. Bold marks a term being defined, not every phrase the writer liked.

## P11 Undefined jargon in a shared document
Use the plain names: competitor, task set, repetitions, attempt, approval rule, answer key,
product under test. Not arm, suite, k, episode, contract, oracle, target,
or audience (there is one report).

## P12 Claims the evidence does not carry
A causal sentence about a model's reasoning is a hypothesis unless an event shows it. Say which
it is.

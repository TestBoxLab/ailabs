    Measurement definitions and limitations
    Trials are zero-based. The selector labels initial repetitions and retries using the run's retained configuration. Log records are observations, not separate attempts: live journals and finalized journals may overlap. Use attempt IDs to group work and log IDs to locate a specific observation. A completed run is not necessarily a pass.
    Tasks are included only after recomputing their hash and matching the run's frozen hash. Outcomes are read from stored results without regrading. Public sharing copies remove credential patterns and provider reasoning. Internal-audience attachments may retain provider summaries. Missing context is not reconstructed.
